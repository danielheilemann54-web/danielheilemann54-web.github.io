GTA6 Clip Autoposter

A personal tool that turns the most-watched Grand Theft Auto VI moments on Twitch into vertical videos and posts them to the owner's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

About · Terms of Service · Privacy Policy

About

Each run looks up the top clips of the last 24 hours in the Twitch category "Grand Theft Auto VI", downloads one clip, crops it to 9:16, adds a short hook line and automatic subtitles, and uploads it.

Every post names the original streamer in its caption as Clip: twitch.tv/<streamer>. Streamers who do not want their clips used are added to a block list and are never posted again.

The tool runs on the owner's computer. It is not a public service, and nobody else can sign in to it.

Terms of Service

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Who can use it

GTA6 Clip Autoposter is used only by its owner, to publish to accounts the owner controls. By connecting an account you confirm that it belongs to you.

What it does with your accounts

With your permission the tool uploads videos and captions to the TikTok, YouTube or Instagram account you connected. It does not follow, like, comment or send messages.

Content and credit

Clips come from public Twitch clips. The original streamer is credited in every caption. Clips are removed on request from the streamer, and the streamer is then blocked from future posts.

Platform rules

All posts must follow the rules of Twitch, TikTok, YouTube and Instagram. The tool may stop working if a platform changes its API or its terms.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is, without any guarantee that uploads succeed or that videos reach a certain audience.

Changes

These terms may change when the tool changes. The date above shows the latest version.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Data the tool uses

  • TikTok: your open ID and basic profile info (user.info.basic), and permission to post videos (video.publish).
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos and to read your channel name.
  • Instagram: permission to publish Reels to your Business or Creator account.
  • Twitch: public clip data such as title, view count and streamer name.

Where it is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a local folder that is never uploaded or shared. A local log lists which clips were posted.

What is not done

No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to build profiles. The tool does not read your followers, messages or viewing history.

Deleting data

You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok, Google or Meta account settings. Deleting the local secrets folder removes all stored tokens.
